# Time Traveler photograph archive

Verified 24 October 2026. The game uses genuine archival photographs. Capture years below come from the linked file descriptions and named originating collections, never from upload timestamps or the dates of depicted products. Facts paraphrase documented scene descriptions; no AI images are used.

Assets are local WebP display derivatives, resized and recompressed with aspect ratio preserved. No generative restoration, colorization, fabricated content, or aggressive crop is applied. Existing archive restorations/tints are identified below. CC BY-SA derivatives retain their source license; public-domain status is recorded as published by the supplying archive. The game UI exposes attribution and the original source; this document links each license.

## Selection and verification

Twenty-six photographs cover 1903–2011 and eleven countries/territories (United States, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Finland, Japan, China, India, Ghana, Kenya, plus Tahiti in French Polynesia). This initial set favors fully documented dates over reaching 50 through uncertain records. Six verified photographs added from 1994–2011 expand everyday life in India and Africa alongside consumer and space technology. Further modern decades and regions can be added after verification.

Each candidate was visually reviewed in a contact sheet; photographs displaying an answer-revealing date were excluded. Two otherwise usable lower-resolution candidates (Woodstock and Sydney) were excluded from the bank to prioritize image quality.
